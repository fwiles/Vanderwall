## FAQ

2. **How much will my case cost?** We charge fixed fees with no hidden costs. You get the exact number for your matter after the consultation, once we know what is actually involved.
3. **Do you offer payment plans?** Yes. Flexible payment plans are available, and we will work out the schedule with you before you sign anything.
4. **What should I bring to the consultation?** Identification, plus any immigration or criminal records for yourself and your family members. If you are not sure whether a document matters, bring it.
