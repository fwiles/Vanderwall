K-1 and I-485 landing page research

Research date: October 7, 2026. This internal handoff is not copied to the public build.

Conversion strategy

The recommendations below are editorial inferences from the legal requirements, not claims of measured conversion lift. Match the search term immediately, connect the service to the visitor’s family plans, explain the decisions an attorney helps with, and keep the first booking step short.

Client source material

Vanderwall fiancé visas establishes help with petition preparation, document collection and later residence. Vanderwall adjustment of status establishes eligibility review, application preparation and interview support. Both identify English/Spanish assistance and Beaverton/Salem offices. The new pages reuse the repository’s existing founder, testimonial and brand sections; those legacy credentials and review counts were not freshly audited.

Copy decisions

  • Do not repeat the client’s fixed 6–9 or 8–14 month estimates. Government processing varies.
  • Do not repeat a single financial sponsorship threshold across K-1 and permanent-residence stages. Requirements differ.
  • Do not promise approval, faster agency processing, or that an attorney can eliminate all delays.
  • Do not describe all J-1 holders as subject to the same restriction, or a challenge to a denial as automatically authorizing continued stay.
  • Separate the free intake appointment from attorney advice. Existing appointment calendars remain the destination.

Several USCIS web pages returned 403/503 during research. Legal points were checked against accessible State Department guidance, the eCFR, and the official USCIS announcement above. No third-party processing-time estimates were used.
