Questions about Military Parole in Place

What is Military Parole in Place?

A discretionary USCIS process: certain family members of active-duty service members, Selected Reserve members and some veterans may request parole while remaining in the U.S.

Who is eligible?

It depends on each family's facts; parole is discretionary, so we review each situation and can't promise approval.

Is there a cost?

Family intake appointments are free; any attorney consultation fee is discussed up front.
