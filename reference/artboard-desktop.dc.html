Immigration is the only law we practice

Green Card

Family petitions and lawful permanent residence, from the first filing through the interview.

Adjustment of Status

Becoming a permanent resident without having to leave the United States.

U.S. Citizenship

Naturalization applications, preparation for the interview, and the oath ceremony.

Deportation Defense

Removal proceedings in immigration court, bond hearings, and cases already on appeal.

ICE Detention Plan

A written plan for your family, your documents and your children — prepared before anything happens.

Asylum

Protection for people who cannot safely return to their home country.

DACA

Renewals and first-time requests for people who were brought here as children.

Fiancé Visas

K-1 petitions and the consular process to bring your fiancé to the United States.

Work Permit

Employment authorization applications, renewals, and problems with a pending card.

U Visa & VAWA

Relief for survivors of crime and of domestic violence, filed confidentially.

T Visa

Protection and status for survivors of human trafficking.

Special Immigrant Juvenile Status

A path to residence for children who have been abused, abandoned or neglected.

Parole in Place

Relief for the spouses, parents and children of U.S. military members and veterans.

Advance Parole

Travel authorization so you can leave and return while your case is still pending.

Yes. Flexible payment plans are available, and we will work out the schedule with you before you sign anything.

Identification, plus any immigration or criminal records for yourself and your family members. If you are not sure whether a document matters, bring it.
