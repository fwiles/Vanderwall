Cases we handle

Immigration is the only law we practice

Which means we have seen your situation before — and the one underneath it.

Green Card

Family petitions and lawful permanent residence, from the first filing through the interview.

Adjustment of Status

Becoming a permanent resident without having to leave the United States.

U.S. Citizenship

Naturalization applications, preparation for the interview, and the oath ceremony.

Deportation Defense

Removal proceedings in immigration court, bond hearings, and cases already on appeal.

ICE Detention Plan

A written plan for your family, your documents and your children — prepared before anything happens.

Asylum

Protection for people who cannot safely return to their home country.

DACA

Renewals and first-time requests for people who were brought here as children.

Fiancé Visas

K-1 petitions and the consular process to bring your fiancé to the United States.

Work Permit

Employment authorization applications, renewals, and problems with a pending card.

U Visa & VAWA

Relief for survivors of crime and of domestic violence, filed confidentially.

T Visa

Protection and status for survivors of human trafficking.

Special Immigrant Juvenile Status

A path to residence for children who have been abused, abandoned or neglected.

Parole in Place

Relief for the spouses, parents and children of U.S. military members and veterans.

Advance Parole

Travel authorization so you can leave and return while your case is still pending.

Questions we get every week

How much is the consultation?

$150. If you retain us within 30 days, that $150 applies toward your case opening fee.

How much will my case cost?

Fixed fees, no hidden costs. You get the exact number for your matter after the consultation, once we know what is actually involved.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Flexible payment plans are available, and we work out the schedule with you before you sign anything.

What should I bring?

Identification, plus any immigration or criminal records for yourself and your family members. If you are not sure whether a document matters, bring it.

What kinds of cases do you take?

Immigration matters only — family petitions, humanitarian relief, naturalization, work authorization and removal defense.

Do you speak Spanish?

Sí. Our team works in English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Mandarin and French — no outside interpreter required.

Can I bring my children?

Children are welcome. That said, if you need to concentrate on difficult details, you may prefer to arrange childcare for the appointment.

Where are you located?

Beaverton and Salem. We represent clients throughout Oregon and Washington State, and clients living abroad.

When are you open?

Monday through Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm Pacific. Closed weekends and most federal holidays. The form above reaches us any hour.
